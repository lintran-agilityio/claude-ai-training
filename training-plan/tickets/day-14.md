# Day 14: Capstone: test and harden

**Labels:** `training` `day-14` `phase::capstone` · **Source:** https://dojo.agilityio.dev/schedule/day-14.html

## Goal
Review and harden the capstone changes before release.

## Tasks
- [ ] Run `/code-review` on your PRs. Use ultrareview if a change touches auth or data.
- [ ] Fix the findings (or dismiss with a reason).
- [ ] Use `/loop` to watch CI on your PRs.
- [ ] Support QA with failing cases and BA with release-note facts.

## Deliverables
- Review reports acted on.
- Green CI on all PRs.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] No unresolved findings.
- [ ] 3 takeaways written.
